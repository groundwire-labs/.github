# Groundwire Labs

**AI you can measure.**

We are an AI consultancy that advises and builds. We treat every AI feature
like an experiment: evidence decides each step.

## How we work

Each stage has to earn the next one.

1. **Before anyone uses it.** Evals on a test set built from your real cases,
   scored against a quality bar we agree up front.
2. **Small, watched release.** Human review, user feedback, and quality and
   cost compared to the bar.
3. **In production.** Monitoring of quality, cost, speed and failures, with
   alerts and repeat evals.

Then we hand it to your team.

## Services

- **Readiness Sprint.** A working prototype of one use case, scored against
  real cases, and the plan to take it live.
- **Build.** We take it through testing and a watched release with real
  users, then hand it to your team.
- **Advisory.** A monthly review of your live AI, your AI roadmap, and help
  hiring an AI team.
